Week 1 Day 2 – pull-ups and capacitors

Open switch ≠ 0 V. Open = floating.
Pull-down: resistor to GND, idle low.
Pull-up: resistor to Vcc, switch to GND, idle high. This is INPUT_PULLUP.
10k is the default. 100 Ω wastes current. 1M is too weak for noise.

Capacitor: I = C dV/dt. Local charge. Buys time. Does not create energy.
Decoupling: 100 nF at the chip for fast spikes. Bulk cap for fat, slow current.
