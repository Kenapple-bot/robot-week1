Week 1 Lesson 5 – 25 Sep – closeout circuit

5V, pull-up 10k + switch to GND → BTN
BTN → 1k → NPN base, emitter GND
5V → 330 → LED → collector
Open: LED on (BTN ~1.1 V with base connected; table says 5 V unloaded).
Closed: BTN=0 LED off.

Divider 1k/2k: 3.33 V, 1.67 mA
Load 2k on tap: 2.50 V, 2.50 mA
Divider senses. It does not power a chip.
